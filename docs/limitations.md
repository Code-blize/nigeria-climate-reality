# Limitations and interpretation

1. Inputs have different native spatial resolutions and levels of detail.
2. Temperature and rainfall use historical periods; flood hazard represents a modelled scenario; population uses a 2025 reference year.
3. Modelled hazard and gridded population products depend on provider assumptions and methods.
4. NASA POWER's coarse native grid limits highly local temperature conclusions.
5. Rainfall CV describes relative variation, not whether rainfall is increasing or decreasing.
6. The JRC layer represents river-flood hazard, not all pluvial, coastal, or drainage-related flooding.
7. Population concentration alone does not show hazard-specific exposure, vulnerability, or adaptive capacity.
8. The indicators are kept separate; no composite risk index is produced.
9. The provenance and licence of `nga_polbnda_adm1_1m_salb` remain to be confirmed.
10. Spatial coincidence between a population layer and hazard layer does not by itself prove exposure, impact, or causation.
